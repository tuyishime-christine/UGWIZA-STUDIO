About UGWIZA STUDIO

UGWIZA Studio is an independent film, television, and multimedia production company headquartered in Kigali, Rwanda. Founded by Uzabumwana Hussein, the studio is dedicated to producing high-quality African stories that entertain, educate, and inspire audiences both locally and internationally.

UGWIZA Studio develops feature films, television series, documentaries, commercials, music videos, photography, and digital media content while discovering and nurturing new creative talent across Rwanda.

Our Purpose

Our Vision

To become one of Africa's leading film and entertainment studios, producing world-class content that represents African stories on the global stage.

Our Mission

Produce high-quality films and television programs. Develop young filmmakers and actors. Promote Rwandan culture through storytelling. Build international partnerships. Create employment opportunities within the creative industry.

Long-Term Goals

Establish a professional film studio in Rwanda. Launch a television channel dedicated to films and entertainment. Build a film academy. Develop a digital streaming platform for African content. Expand across East Africa and international markets.
